## 0. Statement
THEOREM R2 (PROVED(hand)). Let 2 <= e, e+3 <= f, gcd(e,f)=1 (all members, thin and thick). In a tiling by (a,b,c) = (ef, f^2-e^2, f^2) of a triangle with a corner A' = 2 alpha at the left end of a side s (to a corner beta or alpha+beta), alternative (ii) of Theorem S2 with k = e+2 does not occur: i.e. M(e+2, 0) holds at the corner origin P = A'.
Consequence (with M0(e+1), SPLUS, audited SOUND-WITH-FIX): alternative (ii) is impossible for k <= e+2; hence Chisel C1 (W_M(e,f) untileable if M(f-e) < f) is proved for M(f-e) <= e+2 (and e+3 <= f, i.e. M(f-e) < f).
