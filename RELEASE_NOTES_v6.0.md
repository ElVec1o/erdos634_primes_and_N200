# Release v6.0 (18 November 2026)

This version (v6.0, 18 November 2026): doi:10.5281/zenodo.24345081. Previous version v5.0 (1 November 2026): doi:10.5281/zenodo.23857682. All versions (concept DOI): doi:10.5281/zenodo.23400433.

A positive integer N is a tile count if some triangle can be cut into N pairwise congruent triangles (Erdős Problem 634). **The problem is not solved here.** The previous public release is v5.0 of 1 November 2026 (collar construction, up-set theorem at every gamma = 2alpha pair, 117 explicit tile counts). This release adds a theorem that excludes a family of values, with a kernel-checked Lean statement for each excluded value; two families of constructions; 132 explicit tilings; and an updated census. It does not touch the prime case (settled by Paliy on 29 September 2026, by Beeson–Roșu, and independently by us; see v4.0).

**Every claim in this release was checked inside the project only. No reader outside the project has checked any statement, proof, tiling or count.** "Lean (internal audit)" means: the Lean kernel accepted the declaration with axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only (no `sorry`), and a fresh-context internal audit read the formal statement against the stated one. The Lean sources in this repository are sanitized copies of the internal files (comments and docstrings only; the code, comment-stripped and whitespace-normalised, is identical, checked for every added file); the staged tree was not rebuilt here.

Labels: **LEAN (internal audit)**; **LEAN (statement audit, one reader)**; **COMPUTED (exact check, not Lean)**: explicit tilings verified by exact rational checkers with negative controls; **COMPUTED (program output)**: the census lists.

## What is new since v5.0 (1 November 2026), with labels
1. **W-gap theorem. LEAN (internal audit).** For coprime 2 ≤ e < f, f ≤ M e, M(f−e) < f, the triangle W_M(e,f) is not cut into (2f²−e²)M² copies of the tile (ef, f²−e², f²). `WGapRung.no_tiling_W_gap`; rungs M(f−e) ≤ e+2 … e+5: `no_tiling_W_rung2…5`. With the decision rule `not_tileCount_of_CNzFixW` this excludes **5,593 values of N ≤ 10⁶** (5,351 by the theorem, 240 by the rungs, 2 by the rule alone). **For every one of the 5,593 values there is a kernel-checked Lean theorem `¬ TileCount N`**, using only the three standard axioms: the decision predicate is evaluated by the Lean kernel (a memory-light formulation with square-root loops and sound pruning of vacuous ranges, each with its own soundness lemma, `not_tileCount_of_P`), so no census table or program output enters these 5,593 statements. A gate script replayed the axioms of every block (5,593 of 5,593 pass); a comment-aware scan of the import closure (5,062 files) found no `sorry`, `native_decide`, `Lean.ofReduceBool` or `axiom`. The 237 rung values agree with an independent Rust transcription of the same predicate.
2. **Notch seed family and the e = 1 threshold. LEAN (internal audit).** `thetaTiled_all`: for coprime e < f and t ≥ t₀(e,f), the isosceles triangle θ_t(e,f) is tiled, so (f²−e²)t² is a tile count. `thetaTiled_e1_iff`: for e = 1, θ_t is tiled iff t ≥ 2f (the forward direction rests on a module whose audit carries one inherited gap).
3. **Band theorem. LEAN (internal audit).** `thetaTiled_band` (hypotheses (a)–(e), including f ∤ eM), odd-e corollary `thetaTiled_oddE`, alpha hub `alphaTiled_of_thetaTiled`; 1,410 TileCount instances (1,360 + 50).
4. **Structural corner theorems. LEAN (statement audit of 2026-11-16, one reader).** `w_corner_run_long` (every tiling of W_M(e,f), any M, carries f consecutive c-tiles on the side from the 2α corner), `w_bottom_rung_words`, `t_apex_one_leg_long`: they decide no N.
5. **A refuted conjecture. COMPUTED.** The "foot property" FOOT* for tilings of T_M(e,f) is false for M > f: exact check on tilings of T_3(1,2) (N = 99) and T_4(1,2) (N = 176), violators verified by the independent exact checker; it holds in all 252 tilings of T_2(1,2) and in every tiling on file with e ≥ 2 (all with M e ≥ f).
6. **132 new tile counts by explicit tilings. COMPUTED (exact check; not Lean).** 57 by the lift tool (F1 48, F2 9), 15 by Zhang's nesting (F3), 60 by the F1 strip step (17), the lift tool (4) and the F1→Iso transfer (39). Described in the companion data paper; the transfer step is a Lean theorem (`isoTiled_of_f1Tiled`), the seeds are too large for the kernel. The table is `data/registry/registry_tile_counts_TILING_EXISTS_6.0.tsv`; the 50 witnesses of at most 2 MB are in `data/witnesses/explicit_6.0/` (82 larger or recipe-only witnesses are not stored here; the column `witness_in_this_export` says which).
7. **Census.** Since v5.0 (62,372 undecided non-squarefree N ≤ 10⁶, published 1 November) the undecided count fell by exactly **10,327**, to **52,045** (the current list is a subset of the v5.0 list; residual 0): W-gap theorem 5,351; notch/θ/α family 2,694; band theorem and hub 1,410; recovered closures 319; isosceles parity 152; W rungs 2, 3, 4: 77 + 65 + 98; explicit tilings 161 (all after 1 November). Open N ≤ 1000: 27 values (236, 280, …, 999). Every N ≤ 200 is decided (unchanged). The list of the 52,045 undecided values is `data/census/undecided_6.0.txt` (program output of the internal census tool, which is not part of this release).
8. **Papers.** `paper/erdos634_v6_main.pdf` (statements, proof sketches and tables) and `paper/erdos634_v6_companion.pdf` (the data paper on the 132 tilings and the census); sources `.tex` beside them.

## What is NOT claimed
- No claim that the problem is advanced toward completion, or about the open values; no claim about the prime case beyond v4.0 and v5.0.
- No claim of any check by a person outside the project.
- The W-gap theorem does not cover the bottom rung (M(f−e) ≥ f > (M−1)(f−e)), e = 1 or targets other than W_M. The structural theorems decide no value.
- The 132 tilings are not Lean theorems; the census lists are program output (the 5,593 W-gap exclusions are the part of it with per-value Lean statements).
- Formalizations of others' results made since v5.0 (Beeson's lemmas, Beeson–Roșu printed route) are not claimed as ours and are unaudited.

## How to check
Lean (one build at a time; the modules are large): the new leaf modules are collected in aggregates `lean/Erdos634/AllV6a.lean … AllV6g.lean` (library, band instances, W-gap core, link, structural theorems), the root `AllV6.lean`, and for the per-value statements `AllV6h*.lean` (chunked so that each is a single process under 6 GB). They are leaf modules: not imported by `Erdos634.All`. Axioms: after building an aggregate, `lake env lean axioms/AxiomsAllV6b.lean` (also `d`, `e`, `f`, `g`) from `lean/`. Rows of the paper map: `lean/PAPER_MAP_V6.md`. Per-value modules `WGapB*`: the generator, driver, manifest (`MANIFEST_FINAL.md`), gate (`check_manifest.sh`, `GATE_RESULT.txt`) and scan (`scan_closure.sh`, `SCAN_RESULT.txt`) of the internal run are archived in `data/wgap_bulk_2026-11-18/` (the scripts refer to the internal directory layout and do not run here; `SHA256_PUBLIC.txt` has the hashes of the sources in this repository). Census: the list `data/census/undecided_6.0.txt` (52,045 values). Tilings: `data/registry/registry_tile_counts_TILING_EXISTS_6.0.tsv` and `data/witnesses/explicit_6.0/`; they were checked with the project's exact checkers (`bte verify` v2.18.0 and a second exact checker per family), which are not part of this release.
