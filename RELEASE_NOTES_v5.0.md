# Release v5.0, 1 November 2026

A positive integer N is a tile count if some triangle can be cut into N pairwise congruent triangles (Erdős Problem 634). **The problem is not solved here, and this release does not touch the prime case.** Nothing below has been checked by anyone outside the project. Dissections may use reflections of the tile (the convention of the Lean definition `CongruentDissection`).

Labels used in this release:
- **PROVED (hand proof; AI-assisted internal audit; not formalized in Lean)**
- **VERIFIED (Lean, internal audit only; standard axioms; no outside check)**: the Lean kernel accepted the formal statement with axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only, and an internal fresh-context audit of the statement, axioms and proof closure found no defect (used for the collar at (2,3), audit of 29 October 2026, and for the thin-W base lemma, audit verdict SOUND).
- **kernel-checked in Lean (builds with standard axioms propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound); NOT audited**: used for Zhang's 40 instances and the two general Lean statements for his propositions; the comparison of formal and mathematical statement has been made by the author only, and no audit of that file exists.
- **COMPUTED (exact check)**: an explicit tiling checked by the exact rational verifier `bte verify` (congruence of every tile, inclusion, pairwise disjointness, area), with negative controls; the verifier and the generators belong to the project.

## What is new since v4.0 (with labels)

1. **Up-set theorem for the isosceles targets with apex angle 2 alpha. PROVED (hand proof; AI-assisted internal audit; not formalized in Lean).** Let u < v < 2u be coprime integers, let Delta = (u^2, v^2 - u^2, uv) be the tile (its angles are alpha, pi - 3 alpha, 2 alpha, with cos alpha = v/(2u)), b = v^2 - u^2, and let T(g) be the isosceles triangle with legs g u b and base g v b (base angles alpha); a tiling of T(g) uses b g^2 tiles. Put g_D = floor((u-1)/(v-u)) + 1. *Hypotheses:* (i) u, v coprime with u < v < 2u; (ii) g >= g_D; (iii) reflections of the tile are allowed (the construction uses both chiralities and is not valid as written for direct congruences only); (iv) Theorem D': T(g) has no tiling for g <= g_D (Lean theorem `thmD_all_g`, VERIFIED with the label above, in the author's development; not rebuilt in this repository). *Statement:* any tiling of T(g), g >= g_D, extends by an explicit strip of b(2g+1) tiles to a tiling of T(g+1). Hence, given D', the set of g for which T(g) is tiled is {g >= g*} with g_D < g* <= infinity. The proof (cap Delta_u union Delta_v of u^2 + v^2 tiles, C-slices and B-slices, a Frobenius argument for the strip equation a u + b' v = g b - u^2) is in `paper/erdos634_v5_supplement.pdf`. Two scope statements: the strip equation is solvable for g >= g_D and for no g < g_D; that every *minimal* cap has the form Delta_u union Delta_v is **not** proved and is not used. The theorem does not decide for which pairs, or for which g, a first tiling exists.
2. **Collar at (2,3). VERIFIED (Lean, internal audit only; standard axioms; no outside check).** (Internal fresh-context audit of 29 October 2026: statement match, axioms, closure, seed table and chunk coverage all passed; no defect found.) For the tile (4, 5, 6) and every g >= 12, T(g) is tiled by 5 g^2 copies (`collar23`; explicit `CongruentDissection (5 g^2)` in `collar23_dissection`; `collar23_tileCount_all`: 5 g^2 is a tile count for every g >= 12). The only hypothesis is 12 <= g. The seed T(12), 720 tiles, is a layout of B. Herdt, checked by the kernel (`tile23_12`); the step is the collar of item 1 at (u, v) = (2, 3). Consequence: the g for which T(g) is tiled at (2,3) are not confined to multiples of 3 (an earlier guess from small cases); 5 g^2 for g = 13, 14, 16, ... are new in the table of item 4. The modules are in the author's working repository and are to be added to the public Lean repository; they are not rebuilt here (see Checks).
3. **Thin-W base lemma. VERIFIED (Lean, internal audit only; standard axioms; no outside check); internal audit verdict SOUND.** `thinW_base` and `thinW_base_bcount` (arithmetic lemma on the base word of the thin-W regime: for coprime 1 <= e < f and M e < f the decomposition of the base length has the form q = M e, p = xi f, r = 2 M e - xi e with xi e <= 2 M e - f; it is stronger than the printed version in dropping two hypotheses). Four witnesses instantiate it, and a negative control shows that M e < f is needed. This is an arithmetic lemma, not a tiling theorem; the walk-level count for an actual tiling is **not** formalized.

## Observations (not claimed as results)
Our search engine (`bte`, versions 2.1.2, 2.3.1 and 2.4.0, flags band:16, la1, cbj1, ng, no symmetry reduction) ran to completion without finding a tiling on two instances of the base-beta family: T_2(4,5), tile (20, 9, 25), target legs 250 and base 472 (46,313,414 search nodes; reproduced from scratch by a second engine version with the same count); and T_2(3,5), tile (15, 16, 25), target legs 250 and base 396 (192,438,239 nodes in one run, 249,950,358 in a second run with a different cache-eviction policy). No certificate was produced for either; a run with cache eviction is a statement about the engine, not about the instance. Both are consistent with the picture that T_M(e,f) is tiled exactly when M >= f, and neither is used anywhere in this release or in the census. (N = 264 is a tile count by another tile and target, as before.)

## What did not change
The prime case (no prime p congruent to 3 mod 4, p > 3, is a tile count: release of 15 October 2026; dates and parallel works as in v4.0). Every N <= 200 is decided. The band theorem and the base-beta registry extract of v4.0. The scales M with f/(2e) < M < f of the base-beta family remain open beyond what v4.0 states; the full problem is open.

## Not claimed
- No claim of outside verification, of a classification of the tile counts, or of the full problem; nothing here bears on Theorem 1.1 of the main paper.
- The up-set theorem is conditional on D' and on reflections; it does not say that a tiling exists at any particular pair, and it does not say that g* equals g_D + 1. The minimality of the cap is not claimed.
- The engine observations above are not exclusions and are not in the census.
- Search certificates, engines and census program sources are not part of this release.
- The 68 values of item 5 that were already counted as known are not presented as new tile counts of the census.
