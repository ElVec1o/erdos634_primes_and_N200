# Release v3.0 (paper version 3.0), 20 October 2026

Tag `v3.0` covers TWO papers, each of version 3.0: **Paper W** (`paper/erdos634_v3W2.pdf`, 13 pp.: Tilings of W_M and T_M below the threshold scale, a six-piece template; a longer version `erdos634_v3W.pdf` and a verification record `erdos634_v3W_verification.pdf` are kept) and the **companion** (`paper/erdos634_v3C.pdf`, 24 pp.: Conjecture C, the collar family, the thick regime, the census, with computer evidence). Both share one notation table (`paper/notation_table.tex`). The algebra of Theorem W is re-run by `code/check_thmW_algebra.py`. The version 2.2 paper and the first paper are unchanged.

**The prime case is unchanged: no prime p congruent to 3 (mod 4) with p > 3 is a tile count (primes congruent to 1 mod 4 are sums of two squares and are tile counts). Nothing in this release bears on the prime case or on the base-beta question.** This release is about which counts are tile counts and at which scales; it adds a conjecture, a census update, and a list of corrections.

## What is new

1. **Theorem W** (Lean `Draft.ThmW.thmW`; proof, sign table and exact Lean/hand/computed accounting in Paper W). For coprime 2 <= e <= f-2 and an integer M with Me >= f and (M-1)(f-e) >= f, the triangle W_M(e,f) (sides Mf(f^2-e^2), Me(2f^2-e^2), Mf^3) is tiled by M^2(2f^2-e^2) copies of the tile (ef, f^2-e^2, f^2), and the isosceles triangle T_M(e,f) by M^2(3f^2-e^2). The tiling is a layout of six pieces whose scales are explicit functions of M and of n = floor(eM/f). For e < f/2 the condition Me >= f is sharp, because Me < f was excluded in an earlier release. So for e >= 2 the scale f is not the threshold: W_3(2,5) has 414 tiles, W_4(3,5) (sides 320, 492, 500) has 656. This closes the open case W_4(3,5) of the first paper and refutes the size conjecture (longest side >= c^2).
2. **Collar family** (Lean). (f^2-1)(2f^2-1) g^2 for all f >= 2, g >= f+1; 465 g^2 for g >= 5; 2485 g^2 and 966 g^2 for all g >= 2. The up-set in g and the form b(b+c)K^2 are D. Paliy's (2026-09-29); our construction is independent. The collar step with arbitrary height is a hand proof.
3. **27 more tile counts by macro pieces** (Lean): 375, 540, 735, 825, 630, 1120, 1750, 3864, 8694, 9940, 15456, 22365, 24150, 34776, 47334, 61824, 96600, 116886, 139104, 163254, 189336, 217350, 247296, 279174, 312984, 62125, 78246; twelve square multiples of template counts; the thirteen first W tilings below the scale f with closures. 100 exact tilings (`data/witnesses/v3_exact`, 26 MB), verified by the exact verifier of the repository (100/100 including the three 465 g^2 tilings).
4. **Conjecture C**: the set of tile counts is the union of explicit families (Table 1 of the paper); its existence half is a Lean theorem on the proved ranges, the exclusion half is open. Not claimed true.
5. **Census** (non-squarefree N <= 10^6): Lean-formal exclusions / tile counts / undecided = **199,364 / 129,108 / 63,602**; with the hand-proved Theorem R2 class (separate row) 199,441 / 129,108 / 63,525 (was 199,140 / 102,613 / 90,321 in v1.16). Theorem W family: 44,495 values, 26,138 newly known. New tile counts by label: Lean 26,312; exact tiling only 145 (+ the three values above, found after the census run, not counted); hand 35; paper-formal closure 3. 134 values in families claimed by Paliy are not counted. Squarefree open: about 770. Lean-formal exclusions of N <= 1000 by the new chain: 412 and 270.
6. **W3+ census** (evidence only): for g = 3, all 191,940 (4,7) words and all 84,462 (5,8) words are refuted by exact search (conditional on the hand filters); (7,12) is 95.06% covered. No tiling found; not a proof.
7. **Corrections** (companion, Appendix E): 183 is not a tile count; for e >= 2 tilings exist below the scale f (the earlier "threshold = f" pattern is false); the claim that the template exists only when 4f < 9e is false; the size conjecture is false; Paliy's 4830 is a tiling (we had checked the wrong file); the theta statement "te >= f has no tiling" is false (the true bound is te >= 2f); the alpha up-set is Paliy's; two counts in notes.

8. **Thick regime (companion, Section 7).** For coprime 2 <= e < f, M < f, Me >= f: **W_M(e,f) has no tiling if M(f-e) <= e+1** (Lean `Draft.SplusThick.wTilable_not_of_small_M`; the hand proof was audited only inside the project; 124 census values; in the exclusion chain formally: **412 = W_2(5,8)** and, by a different leaf, **270**). **Theorem R2 (HAND PROOF ONLY): the same for M(f-e) = e+2**; proof text in `docs/THEOREM_R2_PROOF.md`; audited twice inside the project by readers of the same model family, tested once by an exact region search at (4,7,2); Lean has only its arithmetic half and a corollary conditional on a statement `R2Count` that is not proved in Lean; NOT read by anyone outside the project; the census row that uses it (+77 values) is kept separate. Lemma W2 (hand): for W_2 with e > f/2 the side OB carries exactly 2(f-e) a-tiles and 2(f-e) c-tiles; the residual window is e+3 <= k <= 2(f-e), smallest cases (5,9), (6,11), (7,12) (open).
9. **465 g^2 for g = 2, 3, 4** (N = 1860, 4185, 7440): tile counts of the tile (4,15,16), base-alpha isosceles targets (240g, 240g, 465g), by exact-verified tilings (`data/witnesses/v3_exact`, verified by `bte verify` and by an independent exact-fraction check). With the Lean-formal tail g >= 5 (Paliy's up-set theorem and our collar from the 11,625 seed) 465 g^2 is a tile count for every g >= 2; g = 2, 3, 4 are exact-only; Lean certificates are planned. The core 465 (g = 1) is open.
10. **Boundary words and a Beatty identity (computed).** Closed form of the three sides of the 52 known template tilings; the threshold of Theorem W as an integer in a Beatty interval, checked on 296,736 triples (identity, not proved here); a paragraph of dead routes with data. Corrections list unchanged.

## What is NOT proved (full list in Section 10 of the paper)

* The **exclusion half of Conjecture C**: no description of the set of tile counts above 200 is proved. 63,602 non-squarefree (63,525 with Theorem R2) and about 770 squarefree values up to 10^6 are undecided.
* The **no side of Theorem W for e > f/2**: for each row at most two scales M (those with (M-1)(f-e) < f < (M+1)(f-e)) are undecided. **e = 1 and e = f-1 for T_M** (first case T_3(1,4)); the T side in general (only 2Me < f is excluded).
* **V and A'** for gamma = 2 alpha (the W3+ evidence is not a proof; (7,12) is incomplete).
* **Theta and S3**: thresholds of the theta-isosceles family and the smallest scales of the base-alpha rows; the squarefree cores 465, 966, 2485 and the values 1860, 4185, 7440.
* **15 m^2 for every m >= 4 as a Lean theorem** (m = 5, 6, 7 are Lean `TileCount` statements; the Lean step `eqD_step` is not yet applied to them); the collar step for arbitrary height (hand); Paliy's claims beyond 4830.
* **An outside check.** All Lean statements, including `theoremP_6` (the prime case of earlier releases) and Theorem W, were compared with the mathematical statements and audited only inside this project. A reading by a person outside the project has not been made. Announcing the prime case as proved remains the author's decision.

## Readability pass (2026-10-19), split into two papers
