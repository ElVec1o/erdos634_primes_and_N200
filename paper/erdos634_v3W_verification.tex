\section{The older proof: complex coordinates and cross-product tables}
The first version of Paper~W and the Lean proof use complex numbers with $O=0$ and $OB$ the positive real axis. With $D=4f^2-e^2$ write $\xi+i\sqrt D\,\zeta$ as $[\xi,\zeta]$ and put
\[
\varepsilon=e^{i\alpha}=\Bigl[\tfrac{\kappa}{2f^2},\tfrac{e}{2f^2}\Bigr],\quad
\eta=e^{i\beta}=\Bigl[\tfrac{e(3f^2-e^2)}{2f^3},\tfrac{b}{2f^3}\Bigr],\quad \omega=\overline{\varepsilon\eta}=e^{-i(\alpha+\beta)},
\]
with $b=f^2-e^2$, $\varepsilon^3\eta^2=-1$, $a+b\omega=c\bar\eta$, $f\omega+e\varepsilon=f\bar\eta$. Then $P=fbM\varepsilon$, and the frame of the paper is $z=P-\xi\,\varepsilon+y\,\omega$. The points are $B=fbM$, $A=f^3M\varepsilon^2$, $P_2=b(fM-et)\varepsilon$, $P_3=P-eat\,\omega$, $Q=P+fbt\,\omega$, $R=P+fbr\,\omega$, $C'=bv\,\varepsilon$, $B'=cv$, $V_2=P+f^3t\,\bar\eta-ef^2r$, $X_4=P-fbq\,\varepsilon$, $U_1=P-ef^2t+ef^2q\,\bar\eta$, $U_2=P-ef^2r+ef^2q\,\bar\eta$ (exact check against the frame: block A). The Lean proof shows that each cross product needed for containment, separation and convexity (about $100$) is a positive constant times a product of sixteen named factors
\begin{gather*}
C_2=r-t,\ C_3=eM-ft,\ C_3'=fM-et,\ C_4=fr-eM,\ C_5=fM-er,\ C_6=fq-er,\ C_6'=fq-et,\\
C_7=ft-eq,\ C_8=f^2t-e^2r,\ C_9=fr-eq,\ C_{10}=M-q,\ E_1=bM+eft,\ E_2=eMf+bt,\ E_3=br+e^2t,\\
L_A=bfM-e\kappa t+f^3q,\ L_B=bfM-e\kappa r+f^3q,\ L_C=efbM-e^2\kappa r+f^4t,
\end{gather*}
all positive for the canonical triple; the tables are generated by \texttt{python3 code/check\_thmW\_algebra.py 60 --tex tables.tex}. Values for three examples (all positive):
